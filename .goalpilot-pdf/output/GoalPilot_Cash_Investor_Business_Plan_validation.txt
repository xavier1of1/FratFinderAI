GOALPILOT
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
01
A purpose-built account for planned purchases
GoalPilot Cash converts intention into an automated funding plan, then keeps the money productive until the user is ready to buy.
Consumers can earmark money at a bank, automate transfers in a budgeting
app, or finance a purchase after the fact. Few products combine the
purchase target, deadline, recurring installment, interest-bearing
account, and completion workflow in one experience.
GoalPilot Cash is designed as a positive alternative to credit-led consumption.
The user chooses a goal, authorizes recurring ACH installments, and receives
a customer-owned deposit account through a regulated partner. GoalPilot
manages the plan and the experience; the partner bank and
embedded-finance provider manage custody, account issuance, interest,
statements, and applicable banking operations.
